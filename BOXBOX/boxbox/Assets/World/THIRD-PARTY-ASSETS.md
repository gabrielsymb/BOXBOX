# World asset provenance

The BOXBOX prototype uses the following Kenney packs. Their source archives and
original license files are retained in `BOXBOX/asset-library/review/Kenney/`.

| Pack | Author | License | Used here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City Kit Roads 2.2 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | roads, signs, lights, construction props, dumpster |
| City Kit Suburban 2.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | suburban buildings and low fence |
| City Kit Commercial 2.1 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | commercial buildings |
| Mini Forest 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | trees, plants and grass patches |
| Furniture Kit 2.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | bench and trash can |
| Car Kit 3.1 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | traffic and driveable vehicle models |

The imported models remain unmodified unless a neighboring `.import` file says
otherwise. Runtime transforms only position, rotate, scale, animate or hide them.
