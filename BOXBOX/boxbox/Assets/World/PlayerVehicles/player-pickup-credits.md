# Player pickup

Pickup-truck-70s by Polyfork (https://polyfork.dev/asset/pickup-truck-70s-8c0080), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original GLB from KIT_CARROS_RETRO in the supplied asset archive. The game rotates, scales and animates this model at runtime.

The textured variant adds BOXBOX material slots and UVs to the original
geometry. Its gray metal and rubber color maps derive from Poly Haven's
[green_metal_rust](https://polyhaven.com/a/green_metal_rust) and
[rubberized_track](https://polyhaven.com/a/rubberized_track) photos, CC0.
