# Poly Haven materials

Downloaded on 2026-11-20 from Poly Haven and used as subtle surface detail in
BOXBOX's curated low-poly assets.

All files listed here are licensed under Creative Commons CC0:

- `asphalt_01`: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_01
- `concrete_floor_worn_001`: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001

The project uses the 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The
original Kenney palette remains responsible for the cartoon colors and road
markings; these maps add restrained wear and material grain.
